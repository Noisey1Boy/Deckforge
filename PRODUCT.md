# PRODUCT.md

## Product purpose
DeckForge AI is a web application that turns a one-sentence brief (or the user's own
data files: CSV, XLSX, JSON, chart screenshots, Mermaid diagrams, markdown) into a
fully designed presentation. It drafts an editable outline first, renders every slide
live from design tokens across 8 curated design systems, computes charts strictly from
user-supplied numbers, and exports editable PPTX, print-ready PDF, standalone HTML,
and a portable .deck.json. Present mode adds speaker notes, a timer, transitions and
a presenter view.

## Users
- Founders, product managers, consultants and analysts who need a credible deck fast.
- Engineers and team leads who present internally (reviews, onboarding, OKRs).
- People with something to say and no time to fight a slide editor.
- They arrive with a prompt or a messy folder of files, and leave with a file they own.

## Brand
DeckForge is a "presentation foundry": opinionated about craft, honest about what AI
does and does not do, allergic to invented data and to template sameness. The voice is
direct, confident, a little theatrical: decks are performances, and this tool builds
the stage. It speaks like a stage manager calling cues: short sentences, concrete
claims, no hype-dust.

## Tone
- Opinionated but never smug. States its rules ("your numbers are sacred") and enforces them in code.
- Concrete over abstract: "editable PPTX", "computed from your numbers", "you approve the outline".
- Warm underneath the discipline; the humor is dry, not jokey.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI-SaaS: purple gradients, sparkle badges, glassmorphism, floating orbs, "Unlock the power of AI".
- The editorial-typographic cliche: display-serif italics + tiny mono labels + hairline rules + monochrome restraint.
- Template-marketplace aesthetics: rows of identical cards, hero-metric stat blocks, stock 3D illustrations.

## Strategic principles
1. Outline before pixels: the user approves the story before any slide is designed.
2. Numbers are sacred: charts come from the user's data or they do not exist.
3. Design systems, not templates: every slide is rendered live from a token set.
4. The file is yours: real exports, no watermarks, no lock-in.
5. Show, don't claim: the landing page's proof is the product rendering real slides live.
