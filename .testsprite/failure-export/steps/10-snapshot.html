The Onboarding Challenge

New customer success hires face a steep learning curve that impacts both their performance and retention.

  • Inadequate onboarding leads to delayed productivity and reduced customer satisfaction
  • High turnover rates among new CS hires increase recruitment costs and disrupt client relat
  • A structured onboarding process accelerates proficiency and improves team performance

The Onboarding Challenge

Before

  • Inconsistent onboarding experiences
  • Extended time-to-productivity
  • High early-attrition rates
  • Knowledge gaps in customer engagement

After

  • Structured, standardized onboarding
  • Accelerated proficiency timeline
  • Improved retention metrics
  • Consistent customer success delivery

The 30-60-90 Model

First 30 Days

  • Master core product knowledge and workflows
  • Complete initial customer portfolio assignments
  • Establish key stakeholder relationships
  • Achieve foundational CS certification

Days 31-90

  • Lead first independent customer reviews
  • Develop and execute retention plans
  • Contribute to strategic initiatives
  • Achieve full CS competency certification

Team Requirements

Current State

  • Extended time-to-productivity impacts team performance
  • Higher turnover due to unclear expectations and support

  • Structured mentorship program with clear milestones
  • Accelerated ramp-up time to full productivity
  • Improved retention through comprehensive support framework
